Statistics, Visuals & Validation 📊

Pages 57-65

Confidence Interval

95% CI = x̄ ± t·s/√n
3.5 → [3.2, 3.8]

Variance Reduction

CRN · Antithetic Variates · Control Variates · Stratified

LLN & CLT

Sample mean → population mean · sums → Normal (n ≥ 30)

Law of Large Numbers · Confidence interval (Pages 57-64)

Roll a fair die n times. The running average approaches the true mean 3.5, and the 95% CI shrinks as n grows.

Central Limit Theorem (Page 57)

Average of 30 die rolls, repeated many times: the histogram becomes a bell curve.
